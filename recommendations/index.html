All Too Well (10 Minute Version)

TAYLOR SWIFT - RED (TAYLOR'S VERSION) - 2021

Melancholic piano meets raw storytelling. The 10-minute version hits different. If you've ever been left behind while the other person moved on, this song gets you.

Story Behind The Song

Taylor was 20 and Jake was 29. They dated for 2 months in 2010.

To her it was a sacred love story. To him it was just a secret fling.

He broke up with her suddenly and mailed her things back, but kept her scarf.

She remembers every detail "I remember it all too well". He forgot.

The song is how she turned that heartbreak into art.
